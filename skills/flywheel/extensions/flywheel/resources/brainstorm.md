# Brainstorm Cadence

Use this when a brief is too fuzzy to spec without guessing.

Ask one question at a time, in this order:

1. What problem is being solved?
2. Who encounters it, and when?
3. What does success look like from that actor's perspective?
4. What constraints shape the solution?
5. What edge cases or failure modes matter?

Rules:

- Do not suggest solutions during discovery.
- Do not batch questions.
- Do not write the spec until the problem, success, constraints, and major assumptions are
  clear.
- If the user wants to proceed with uncertainty, record the uncertainty in `Open Questions`
  so validation can flag it.
